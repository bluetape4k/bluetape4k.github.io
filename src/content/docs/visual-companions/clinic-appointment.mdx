---
title: Clinic Appointment Visual Companions
description: Interactive, bilingual companions for understanding approved clinic appointment designs and their history.
---

These visual companions make the clinic appointment service's more intricate product and booking rules easier to inspect without replacing the normative Markdown designs.

The published set contains six bilingual companions. The two foundation companions use `hybrid`; the profile and Issue #184 product companions use `simulation`:

- `simulation` explains how the approved design behaves under different choices.
- `history` is available on the two foundation companions to show why those designs reached their current shape.

The published files are offline snapshots pinned to clinic commit [`e974333`](https://github.com/bluetape4k/clinic-appointment/commit/e9743337cdc1bf499af68e4e94a9f06b2833d838). Their bytes are checked during every Pages build.
