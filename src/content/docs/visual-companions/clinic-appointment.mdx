title: Clinic Appointment Visual Companions
description: Interactive, bilingual companions for understanding approved clinic appointment designs and their history.
---

These visual companions make the clinic appointment service's more intricate business rules easier to inspect without replacing the normative Markdown designs.

Both current companions use the `hybrid` presentation mode:

- `simulation` is the default view and explains how the approved design behaves.
- `history` shows why the design reached its current shape and links the supporting changes.

The published files are offline snapshots pinned to clinic commit [`6967e38`](https://github.com/bluetape4k/clinic-appointment/commit/6967e385bf92a40619a9bbe17f96da182ac8bd50). Their bytes are checked during every Pages build.
