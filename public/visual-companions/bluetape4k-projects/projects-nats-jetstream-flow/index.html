Adapter owns

The adapter owns the cold bridge, bounded channel, pending/drop observation, blocking-receive interruption, and cleanup of the JetStreamSubscription or IterableConsumer it created.

Caller owns

The caller owns connection lifecycle, stream and durable-consumer creation, PublishAck interpretation, ack/nak/term, retry/backoff, idempotency, metrics, and external authorization.
